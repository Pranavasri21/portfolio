  • MQ2 Smoke Sensor
  • Gas Sensor
  • Flame Sensor
  • PIR Motion Sensor
  • Buzzer, LEDs, Resistors
  • GSM Module (SIM800)

Software & Tools: Arduino IDE, Embedded C/C++

Outcome: Successfully detected smoke and fire conditions and triggered alarms, improving home safety and emergency response.
